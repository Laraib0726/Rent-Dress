Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 15% discount for 4+ day rentals work?

Whenever you select a return date that is more than 3 days after your start date, our system automatically applies an instant 15% discount to your calculated rental fee.

Can I select custom start and return dates?

Yes. You are free to choose any start date and any return date according to your event schedule.

When and how is my security deposit refunded?

Once the courier picks up the outfit on your return date and quality inspection completes, your 100% security deposit is credited back to your account within 24 hours.
